# VLS-TS Validation

## Summary

Non-EU students who arrive in France with a VLS-TS must validate their visa online after arrival.

The validation should be treated as one of the first urgent post-arrival steps.

The student should not confuse having a visa sticker with completing the post-arrival validation process.

## Key facts

- VLS-TS means "visa long séjour valant titre de séjour".
- The validation process is online.
- The student must validate the VLS-TS within 3 months of arriving in France.
- The student needs a valid email address.
- The student needs information from the visa.
- The student needs their date of arrival in France.
- The student needs a home address in France.
- The student needs a way to pay the residence-permit tax/fee online or through an electronic stamp.

## Roadmap implication

If the student has a VLS-TS and has not validated it, the roadmap should mark "Validate VLS-TS" as urgent.

If the student has not arrived yet, the roadmap should mark this as a future post-arrival step.

If the student does not know whether they validated it, the roadmap should mark the status as unknown and recommend checking the official portal or validation confirmation email.

## Agent phrasing

"Because you arrived with a VLS-TS, visa validation is one of your first important steps. If you have not validated it yet, you should prioritize it. You will usually need your visa information, arrival date, French address, email, and payment method or electronic stamp."

## Do not say

- "Your legal status is definitely valid."
- "You are definitely in an irregular situation."
- "I can validate your visa for you."
- "You do not need to verify this officially."
