# Campus France Arrival Checklist

## Summary

A non-EU student arriving in France has several post-arrival tasks that depend on each other.

The roadmap should not treat all tasks as equal. Some tasks are urgent, some are blocked by missing documents, and some are useful dependencies for later steps.

## Key facts

- A student with a VLS-TS should validate the visa soon after arriving.
- VLS-TS validation must be completed within 3 months after arrival.
- Non-European students must register for the French social security system.
- Health-insurance registration is done online through the foreign-student Ameli portal.
- Health-insurance registration should happen after registration at the institution and after visa validation.
- A bank account/RIB is useful for reimbursements, rent, CAF, and monthly payments.

## Roadmap implication

The roadmap should usually order the steps like this for a newly arrived non-EU student:

1. Validate VLS-TS.
2. Complete CVEC and university registration.
3. Register for Ameli.
4. Open bank account / get RIB.
5. Prepare CAF if the student has housing and wants housing aid.
6. Track residence renewal for later.

## Agent phrasing

"I’ll organize your steps as a roadmap. Some steps are urgent, like visa validation. Some steps are blocked by university registration or documents. Others, like CAF, usually become relevant once you have housing and a RIB."

## Do not say

- "Every student has the exact same order."
- "You can skip official verification."
- "I can complete the steps for you."

## User questions this document should answer

- What should I do after arriving in France?
- What comes first: visa validation, university registration, Ameli, bank, or CAF?
- Why is Ameli blocked until university registration and visa validation?
- Why is a RIB important?
