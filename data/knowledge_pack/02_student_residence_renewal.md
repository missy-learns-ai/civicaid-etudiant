# Student Residence Renewal

## Summary

For non-EU students staying in France beyond the first year, residence renewal timing must be tracked early.

The student should not wait until the visa or residence document is almost expired.

## Key facts

- A student VLS-TS is generally valid between 4 months and 1 year.
- After the first year, a student may need to request a student residence permit.
- The renewal request should be made online.
- The renewal window is from 4 months before expiry to 2 months before expiry.
- The roadmap should create a future reminder if the student knows their visa expiry date.

## Roadmap implication

If `visa_expiry_date` is known, calculate:

- renewal_window_start = visa_expiry_date minus 4 months
- renewal_window_end = visa_expiry_date minus 2 months

If the expiry date is unknown, mark the renewal step as unknown and ask the student to check the expiry date on their visa or residence document.

## Agent phrasing

"Your renewal is not necessarily something to do today, but it is important to track. If you know your visa expiry date, I can calculate the recommended renewal window."

## Do not say

- "Your renewal will definitely be approved."
- "You can ignore renewal until the last month."
- "This is legal advice."
- "You do not need to check the official portal."

- When should I renew my student residence document?

- How early should I prepare for renewal?
- What happens if I do not know my visa expiry date?
