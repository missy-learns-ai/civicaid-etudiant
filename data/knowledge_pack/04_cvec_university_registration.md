# CVEC and University Registration

## Summary

CVEC stands for "Contribution de vie étudiante et de campus".

For many students in initial higher education, a CVEC attestation is required before administrative registration at the institution.

The student may either pay the CVEC or receive an exemption, depending on their situation. In both cases, the key item for the roadmap is the CVEC attestation.

## Key facts

- The CVEC attestation is required for registration in initial higher education.
- The student needs the attestation to complete administrative registration.
- Completing administrative registration can unlock other documents, such as the certificat de scolarité or student card.
- The certificat de scolarité or student card may be needed for later steps such as Ameli and banking.

## Roadmap implication

If `cvec_status = not_done`, mark university registration as blocked or in progress.

If the student has not completed university registration, Ameli should usually be marked blocked because Ameli depends on institutional registration.

If the student has a certificat de scolarité or student card, mark university registration as done or mostly done, depending on the rest of the profile.

## Agent phrasing

"CVEC is important because the attestation is usually needed for administrative registration at your institution. Once your university registration is complete, you can usually access documents such as your certificat de scolarité, which helps with later steps."

## Do not say

- "Every single student must pay CVEC."
- "You are definitely exempt."
- "I can pay CVEC for you."
- "Your university registration is officially complete."
