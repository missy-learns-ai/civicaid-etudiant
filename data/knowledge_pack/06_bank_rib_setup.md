# Bank Account and RIB

## Summary

Opening a bank account is not a government procedure, but it is an important dependency for student life in France.

A RIB is frequently useful for reimbursements, rent payments, CAF, subscriptions, and other monthly payments.

## Key facts

- Foreign students can open a bank account in France.
- Common documents needed are identification, proof of residence, and an attestation of enrollment or student card.
- If the student does not yet have accommodation, they may be able to use the address of the international relations office of their institution.
- After opening a bank account, the student receives a RIB.
- A RIB helps with monthly payments and administrative processes.

## Roadmap implication

If `has_bank_account = true` and `has_rib = true`, mark bank/RIB as done.

If the student has university registration or student card and proof of residence, mark bank/RIB as ready.

If the student lacks proof of residence and student certificate, mark bank/RIB as blocked or partially ready.

If the student wants CAF and has no RIB, mark CAF as blocked by RIB.

## Agent phrasing

"A RIB is not a government step by itself, but it unlocks many other processes. It is useful for reimbursements, rent payments, CAF, and monthly payments."
